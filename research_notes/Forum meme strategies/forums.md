# Forum meme strategies

Scope: public pages from 2024 through 8 Oct 2026, excluding Reddit. Goal was mechanical buy/sell or refuse rules a simulator can replay. Skipped “follow my calls” rooms, and skipped pages whose useful content was how to bundle, manufacture volume, or snipe. Short quotes only.

Seven methods below carry numbers. They are screens people publish, not measured edges. The same metric (dev hold, top 10) is capped anywhere from about 3% to 40% depending on the writer.

## Graduation / migration

### Takeaway

Published entries cluster in three bands: a tiny activity floor near 5% curve progress, a mid-curve window around 50–70 SOL or 50–95% progress, and a late “about to graduate” screen above 80%. The only explicit exit tied to migration is to sell into graduation because the first minutes after the pool opens are described as a 30–50% dump. A fixed dollar market cap is not the 2026 rule; later writeups use about 85 SOL, and the dollar figure moves with SOL.

### Cited Findings

**Method 1 — Photon conservative, momentum, and early presets.** Site: PhotonGuides. Date of the interface read: 6 Aug 2026. No separate publication date on the page.

- Conservative start: bonding-curve progress above 80%, top-10 holders under 30%, developer holding under 5%, holder count above 150, and a liquidity floor “at roughly ten times your intended position.” The author says this buys continuation after others have already found the token. — [Photon Memescope: What Every Filter Means (2026)](https://photonguides.com/guides/trading/photon-memescope-guide)
- Momentum start: progress between 50% and 95%, “rising volume,” a buy-side skew, top-10 under 40%, socials not required. The same page says this is “the pattern that is cheapest to fake,” and tells the reader to write an exit before entering. No numeric buy/sell ratio. — [PhotonGuides](https://photonguides.com/guides/trading/photon-memescope-guide)
- Early start: progress under 20%, developer holding under 3%, top-10 under 50%, “a minimal liquidity floor,” and “small uniform position sizes across many attempts” because “most of these go to zero.” — [PhotonGuides](https://photonguides.com/guides/trading/photon-memescope-guide)
- A band “from roughly 85 to 99 percent” is described as tokens about to complete, “where the crowd is already looking.” At 100% the curve fills and liquidity migrates to an AMM. Since March 2025, migrations go to PumpSwap rather than Raydium. — [PhotonGuides](https://photonguides.com/guides/trading/photon-memescope-guide)
- One live read of the default feed that day, nine rows, not a rule: About to Graduate showed market caps about $13K–$14K and volume about $34K–$86K; Graduated showed market caps about $22K–$60K with similar volume. Top-10 share “barely moves” across Newly Created, About to Graduate, and Graduated. — [PhotonGuides](https://photonguides.com/guides/trading/photon-memescope-guide)
- FAQ on the same page: top 10 at “60 percent or higher” means a handful of wallets can sell into the floor; “under 20 percent” on a minutes-old token can be many small buyers or one actor spread across wallets. — [PhotonGuides](https://photonguides.com/guides/trading/photon-memescope-guide)

**Method 2 — Frank (frankdegods) early floor, Photon Memescope.** Clip date: 2 Dec 2024. Recap page sources dated 7 Oct 2026. Site: apebrief, summarizing a public clip.

- “5% pump progress and holder count 5.” Snipers filtered out. Quoted reason: “minus EV to buy coins that have obvious snipers.” An age-in-minutes filter on the about-to-graduate column, with no number given. — [Best Photon Memescope Filters](https://apebrief.com/photon/memescope-filters/)
- The recap says the screen has changed since and to “take the idea, not the numbers.” Graduating cards can sit for hours or more than a day (raw capture 7 Oct 2026: ages from 20m to 1d 15h). — [apebrief](https://apebrief.com/photon/memescope-filters/)

**Method 3 — Curve phases in SOL, sell at graduation.** Site: J Tools editorial. Date: 4 Sep 2026.

- Graduation when the pool holds about 85 SOL. Until March 2025 the migration went to Raydium, “minus a 6 SOL migration fee”; after that, PumpSwap. LP is burned. — [Pump.fun Bonding Curve Mechanics](https://j.tools/en/blog/pump-fun-bonding-curve-mechanics-explained)
- “Ideal entry: phases 2–3 (5–25 SOL),” if holder distribution is widening. “Safer entry: phase 4 (50–70 SOL), if graduation looks near-certain.” The page says the safer window’s profit “rarely” exceeds 5–10x. — [J Tools](https://j.tools/en/blog/pump-fun-bonding-curve-mechanics-explained)
- Exit: “many traders sell at graduation. The first 5 minutes after graduation see dump volatility; price often drops 30–50%.” Holders who stay are told the dump “clears within 24 hours.” “50%+ drawdowns” in the first 24 hours are also asserted. “Plenty of tokens stall in the high seventies and drift back down.” — [J Tools](https://j.tools/en/blog/pump-fun-bonding-curve-mechanics-explained)
- Base rates on the same page, not entry rules: “roughly 2–3% graduate”; “97–98%” never graduate; “about 70% of tokens fail to clear” the 5–25 SOL phase. These percentages are not tied to a named dataset on the page.
- The page calls the curve quadratic. Pump.fun’s own docs describe a constant-product curve, so the formula label should not be replayed. The 85 SOL threshold is the part that matches other 2025–2026 writeups. — [J Tools](https://j.tools/en/blog/pump-fun-bonding-curve-mechanics-explained); [Pump.fun bonding curve docs](https://pump.fun/docs/bonding-curve)

**Older dollar threshold, not a 2026 rule.** A Cointelegraph piece carried on TradingView says that once a token reached a $63,000 market cap the curve completed and the token launched on Raydium, and that creators were to be paid 0.5 SOL (about $80) on completion. The excerpt dates the team post to Aug. 9 and does not state the year in the snippet used here; the venue is Raydium, which later writeups place before March 2025. Full article was not opened. — [TradingView / Cointelegraph](https://www.tradingview.com/news/cointelegraph:555c8df51094b:0-pump-fun-to-give-80-if-tokens-complete-bonding-curve-will-it-stop-rugs/)

### Inferences

- A simulator can treat “buy” as three separate policies rather than one: early (progress ≥ 5% and holders ≥ 5, or progress < 20% with dev < 3% and top 10 < 50%), mid (50–70 SOL in the pool, or progress 50–95%), and late (progress > 80% or 85–99%, with the conservative holder and liquidity gates).
- The migration itself is used as an exit by the J Tools writeup, and as an entry zone by the Photon conservative preset. Those are opposite trades on the same event.
- Dollar market-cap triggers from the Raydium era ($63k in the Cointelegraph account) should not be hardcoded for 2026 replays. SOL-in-pool or curve percent travels better.

### Gaps

- No fetched page gave a rule of the form “sell 100% in the migration transaction” versus “sell X% and hold the rest,” with a sample of trades.
- The 30–50% post-graduation drop and the “clears within 24 hours” line are editorial. No table or query is cited on that page.
- BullX “migration sniping” filter numbers (market cap and volume floors, max age) appeared in a search snippet for bullxneo.net. The page returned HTTP 500, so those numbers are not used.
- Bitcointalk threads that came up were either scam-rate commentary or advertisements for ranking bots and bundled launches. They were not used as plays.

## Dev wallet

### Takeaway

The tight public caps are “do not buy if the dev holds more than about 3–5%.” A looser product default highlights dev hold at 30%. One named Axiom setup leaves dev holding unset on purpose. “Dev already sold” is not a single signal: one checklist treats an immediate 80%+ sale as a red flag, and a Photon guide says a zero balance can mean the creator already moved the coins.

### Cited Findings

**Method 4 — Dolan Duck holder checklist, including dev.** Site: dolanduck.io. Date: 27 April 2026. Commercial explainer; thresholds are stated as practice, not as a counted sample.

- Dev above 5% is a “direct rug pull vector.” Green flag: below 2%, “or already sold small position gradually.” Red flag for activity: “Dev sold 80%+ immediately.” Green: “Dev holds small position, no sells.” — [Top Holders on Pump.fun](https://dolanduck.io/blog/top-holders-pump-fun/)
- The dev is described as the first buyer on the bonding curve. “A dev who has already sold everything before graduation is the most common rug pattern.” — [Dolan Duck](https://dolanduck.io/blog/top-holders-pump-fun/)

**Photon dev caps (same presets as Method 1).** Under 5% in the conservative setup, under 3% in the early setup. A holding of “10 percent or more” is called the classic dump. Zero “is not automatically the clean outcome” because the creator may have moved the position. — [PhotonGuides](https://photonguides.com/guides/trading/photon-memescope-guide)

**Method 5 — Luke Giroud’s Axiom caps, dev left blank.** Summarized by apebrief. Page sources dated 7 Oct 2026. The video is described as his “September setup video”; the page does not give the video’s year.

- He “leaves Dev Holding % out of his filters” because it “moves with the meta,” and it should be read with the other rows rather than alone. — [How to Check If a Memecoin Is a Rug on Axiom](https://apebrief.com/axiom/rug-check/)

**Method 6 — GMGN highlight defaults and a binary “still holding” flag.**

- Trenches Metrics Threshold defaults, read on a GMGN blog against a token page on 19 Aug 2026: Top 10 hold and DEV default to 30%. The blog says these “are defaults rather than standards” and “not a safety promise.” — [What to Check on GMGN After You Find a Trending Memecoin](https://gmgn.ai/blog/what-to-check-after-finding-a-trending-memecoin/)
- GMGN skills risk checklist (file itself undated): `creator_token_status` of `creator_hold` means “dev still holds, dump risk active.” The sample scorecard marks dev holding as “Sold” versus “Still holding,” with no percent on that line. — [workflow-risk-warning.md](https://github.com/GMGNAI/gmgn-skills/blob/main/docs/workflow-risk-warning.md)

### Inferences

- For a replay, “max dev hold 5%” is the number that shows up as an actual buy gate in more than one place (Dolan Duck and the Photon conservative preset). The 3% early-Photon gate is stricter. The 30% GMGN figure is a UI highlight default, and the blog tells the reader not to treat it as a standard.
- A “dev sold” exit is not well specified. The usable mechanical versions are: skip if current dev percent is above the cap; skip if the creator sold 80% or more immediately (Dolan Duck); do not treat dev percent at zero as a pass (PhotonGuides).

### Gaps

- No verified page gave a sell trigger such as “exit when dev sells X% of their remaining bag after I am in.”
- Luke’s choice to leave the filter empty is a refusal to use a fixed percent, not a measured optimal cap.
- Reddit threads on dev-hold percent were out of scope and not used.

## Bundles and snipers

### Takeaway

Refusal lines that are actually numbers: GMGN’s danger line at a bundler rate above 30% and a top-10 rate above 50%; GMGN’s Trenches highlight defaults at 10% for bundlers and snipers; Luke Giroud’s cap of 40% on top 10, snipers, insiders, and bundle together. Axiom’s published counting rule is four or more transactions in one block. Photon, as documented in Aug 2026, had no bundle detector; by 7 Oct 2026 its filter panel exposed sniper count and sniper-holder percent, but the public clip that uses them does not give a max count.

### Cited Findings

**GMGN danger lines (part of Method 6).** Undated skills doc.

- `bundler_rate` above 0.3: “heavily bot-bundled at launch, artificial price support.” `top_10_holder_rate` above 0.5: “extreme concentration.” `rat_trader_amount_rate` above 0.3: insider trading. `rug_ratio` above 0.3. `is_wash_trading` true means volume is fake. — [workflow-risk-warning.md](https://github.com/GMGNAI/gmgn-skills/blob/main/docs/workflow-risk-warning.md)
- Scorecard on the same page: top 10 under 20% marked acceptable, 20–50% a watch, above 50% a stop. Separate warning: top 1–3 wallets holding more than 20% combined means one exit can crash the price. Wallets tagged `bundler` or `rat_trader` among top holders are an insider-concentration flag. — [workflow-risk-warning.md](https://github.com/GMGNAI/gmgn-skills/blob/main/docs/workflow-risk-warning.md)
- A documented preset named `safe` applies `max_rug_ratio=0.3`, `max_bundler_rate=0.3`, and `max_insider_ratio=0.3`. This is from the skills search excerpt for `workflow-early-project-screening.md`; that file was not fetched in full, so treat the preset line as secondary to the risk-warning table above. — [gmgn-market skill](https://github.com/GMGNAI/gmgn-skills/blob/main/skills/gmgn-market/SKILL.md)

**GMGN Trenches defaults (part of Method 6).** 19 Aug 2026 blog.

- Defaults: Top 10 and DEV 30%; Insiders and Phishing 5%; Bundlers, Fresh Wallet, Sniper, and Rugged 10%. Again, defaults, not a promised safe zone. — [GMGN blog](https://gmgn.ai/blog/what-to-check-after-finding-a-trending-memecoin/)
- Snipers on the token page are “the earliest addresses to buy.” The popover is titled “First 70 buyers,” with states Hold, Sell Partial, Buy More, Sell All, and Transferred. On the example token, 65 of the first 70 had sold all. That is a case study, not a threshold. — [GMGN blog](https://gmgn.ai/blog/what-to-check-after-finding-a-trending-memecoin/)
- GMGN’s bundle explainer says to read Bundle % and whether bundler wallets are still holding, and to cross-check DEV, Sniper, Insider, and Top 10. It does not publish a refuse-to-buy percent. — [What Is a Meme Coin Bundle?](https://gmgn.ai/blog/what-is-a-meme-coin-bundle/)

**Luke Giroud 40% cap (Method 5).** apebrief, sources 7 Oct 2026.

- “Cap top 10, snipers, insiders and bundle at 40%, then do his own checks on whatever passes.” He keeps it loose “because on a coin minutes old, Axiom can count plain early buyers as snipers.” — [apebrief Axiom check](https://apebrief.com/axiom/rug-check/)
- After a coin “opened on one huge candle,” wait for Snipers H. to fall and Holders to climb before buying. A coin that “went straight up to 12K in a single candle” is called obviously bundled, with bundler warnings “all on block zero.” — [apebrief](https://apebrief.com/axiom/rug-check/)
- Axiom’s own rule, as restated there: four or more transactions in the same block count as a possible bundle; a wallet is dropped if its next transaction is not bundled. Bundles are counted at any time, not only at launch, and need not be the dev. — [apebrief](https://apebrief.com/axiom/rug-check/)

**Frank sniper exclusion (Method 2).** No sniper count or sniper-hold percent. The instruction is to filter “obvious” snipers. As of a 7 Oct 2026 screenshot, Photon’s Audit tab has min/max boxes for Snipers and Snipers Holder (%), plus Insiders and Insider Holder (%). — [apebrief Photon filters](https://apebrief.com/photon/memescope-filters/)

**Conflict on whether Photon even has snipers.** PhotonGuides, from documentation read 6 Aug 2026, says “nothing called Sniper” appears in Photon docs and that bundle detection is not a documented Photon feature. The Oct 2026 apebrief screenshot shows Snipers and Snipers Holder (%) in the product UI. The UI is the later source. — [PhotonGuides](https://photonguides.com/guides/trading/photon-memescope-guide); [apebrief](https://apebrief.com/photon/memescope-filters/)

**Dolan Duck concentration and bundle shape (Method 4).** 27 Apr 2026.

- Top 10 above 35% is the red flag; below 20% is the green flag. Single wallet above 10% red, below 3% green. “No single wallet should hold more than 3–5%,” and top 10 “under 20%,” in the FAQ. — [Dolan Duck](https://dolanduck.io/blog/top-holders-pump-fun/)
- Bundle red flag: “3+ wallets with identical buy times.” The page says sniper bots often take 3–8% before a human trades, and that 4–5 wallets at 4% each, all in block 1, are one entity at 20%. Those are illustrations, not a bundler-rate formula. — [Dolan Duck](https://dolanduck.io/blog/top-holders-pump-fun/)

### Inferences

- A simulator that needs one bundler refuse-line from an official GMGN checklist should use bundler rate above 30%, and top 10 above 50% as the extreme bin. The 10% Trenches default is a highlight, and Luke’s 40% is a personal loose cap that he says still needs a manual pass.
- “Sniper count” almost never appears as a number in these sources. The mechanical substitutes are: exclude tokens Photon or the trader labels as obvious snipers (Frank); cap sniper holding percent at 40% (Luke); highlight sniper holding at 10% (GMGN default).
- Identical buy timestamps, or four buys in one block, are the counting definitions. Replaying them needs slot or block data, not just a holder-percent column.

### Gaps

- No fetched trader writeup said “refuse if sniper count is greater than N” with N filled in, other than the unverified BullX snippet (max snipers 7), which was dropped because the page failed to load.
- GMGN’s Bundle % blog never states the percent at which to refuse.
- How vendors compute “bundler rate” (share of launch volume versus share of current supply) is not consistent across GMGN, Axiom, and Dolan Duck. A replay should not mix those fields under one name.

## Momentum

### Takeaway

Numeric momentum gates that survived a full-page read are small: at least 5 holders and 5% curve progress (Frank), holder-count floors of 150 in the Photon conservative preset, and Dolan Duck’s “under 30 holders at 1 hour” versus “200+.” Volume shows up as “rising,” as a liquidity floor under $10,000 being extreme, or as “5–10 wallets dominate, so the volume is fake.” No verified page gave a buy/sell ratio such as 2:1 or a unique-buyer minimum stated as a tested edge.

### Cited Findings

**Frank activity floor (Method 2).** Minimum bonding progress 5% and holder count 5, so untouched coins drop out while early coins still show. — [apebrief](https://apebrief.com/photon/memescope-filters/)

**Photon momentum preset (Method 1).** Requires rising volume and buy-side skew inside the 50–95% progress band, with top 10 under 40%. No ratio, no dollar volume, no unique-buyer count. Holder count “on its own is close to meaningless” because wallets are cheap; it only helps when read against volume. Buy/sell skew “is the easiest metric on the entire dashboard to manufacture.” — [PhotonGuides](https://photonguides.com/guides/trading/photon-memescope-guide)

**Photon conservative holder floor (Method 1).** More than 150 holders, plus liquidity about 10× the intended position. The 6 Aug 2026 feed snapshot showed Newly Created volume around $1.2K–$3.6K versus about $34K–$86K once tokens were in About to Graduate. That is a description of one session, not a threshold the author tells you to copy. — [PhotonGuides](https://photonguides.com/guides/trading/photon-memescope-guide)

**Dolan Duck holder-age gates (Method 4).** Under 30 unique holders at 1 hour old is a red flag; 200+ is a green flag. Under 50 holders on a 30-minute-old token is “thin”; under 20 is “extremely concentrated.” An organic example given in prose: 500+ holders, top wallet 2%, top 10 combined 12%. — [Dolan Duck](https://dolanduck.io/blog/top-holders-pump-fun/)

**J Tools fake-volume rule (Method 3).** “If 5–10 wallets dominate, you’re looking at fake volume.” No buy/sell ratio. — [J Tools](https://j.tools/en/blog/pump-fun-bonding-curve-mechanics-explained)

**GMGN liquidity floor (Method 6).** Current liquidity under $10,000 is “extreme exit slippage risk.” A drop of more than 30% versus a prior reading is a warning. Volume spikes without a price increase are called selling pressure absorbing buys. No unique-buyer threshold in the fetched risk doc. — [workflow-risk-warning.md](https://github.com/GMGNAI/gmgn-skills/blob/main/docs/workflow-risk-warning.md)

**Cented, process not a metric.** Posted 26 Jul 2026, via apebrief: beginners should paper trade and “watch the scope all day” rather than copy a filter set. No percents. — [apebrief](https://apebrief.com/photon/memescope-filters/)

### Inferences

- The replayable momentum rules are floors on activity (5 holders, or 150, or 200 by one hour) plus a qualitative “volume is rising and not from 5–10 wallets.” They are not ratio strategies.
- Dollar volume from the Photon feed snapshot ($1K–$4K when newly created, tens of thousands near graduation) is a scale check for 6 Aug 2026, not a published entry.

### Gaps

- No Medium, Substack, Bitcointalk, TradingView idea, Birdeye doc, or BullX/Photon/Axiom page that was fully fetched stated a buy/sell count ratio, a unique-buyer minimum, or a dollar-volume minimum as the entry itself. Birdeye’s holder-profile docs describe bundler and sniper tags; they do not give a trader’s cutoff.
- A BullX tutorial snippet listed minimum volume $5,000–$50,000, minimum holders 50–100, and an example of $5,000 volume with 50 holders. The URL failed with HTTP 500, so it is not a cited method.
- Pages about volume bots and “volume between 40% and 70% of the curve” were excluded because they describe manufacturing activity, not a rule for buying someone else’s coin.
